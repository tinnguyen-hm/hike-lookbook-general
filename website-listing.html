HIKE

Custom Insoles

Provided byHike Medical

Your [Client] benefits cover a pair of Hike Custom Insoles, every pair — no doctor appointments, no paperwork.

When your feet aren’t properly supported, your knees absorb extra stress, your hips compensate, and your lower back tightens to pick up the slack — fatigue that follows you through the rest of the day. Custom insoles are built to match your exact foot shape, helping your body move the way it’s meant to instead of pushing that strain up your skeleton.

Insoles from the store are one-size-fits-all. Your feet deserve insoles made just for you. Getting custom insoles through a specialist means appointments, months of waiting, and costs that can reach $500 to $1,000. Hike builds your medical-grade custom insoles from a 5-minute scan on your phone and delivers them to your door in two weeks.
